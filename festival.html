About Durga Puja

Durga Puja is one of the most important Hindu festivals. It celebrates the victory of Goddess Durga over the demon Mahishasura.

The festival represents the victory of good over evil and the triumph of righteousness.

Religious Significance

Goddess Durga is worshipped as a symbol of divine power, strength and protection.

Devotees gather in temples and community celebrations to offer prayers and participate in religious rituals.

Puja Rituals

The worship of Goddess Durga includes prayers, offerings, devotional songs and various traditional rituals.

The exact rituals and timings may vary according to regional traditions and local Panchang.
